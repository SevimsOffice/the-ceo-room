# Open questions (Phase 0): need the user's answer before Milestone 1

1. **Company name.** Spec: "NOVARA Industries". Design: "NOVARA Sanayi". Localise the name (EN Industries / TR Sanayi) or keep one name in both languages?
2. **Cash runway definition.** Design: 41.2M TRY cash ≈ 52 days. With ~75M/month revenue, operating costs are ~70M/month (~2.3M/day), which gives about 18 days if runway = cash ÷ daily costs. Options: (a) cash ÷ average daily *net* outflow, (b) cash ÷ daily operating costs, (c) days until projected cash < 0 from the engine forecast. Recommended: (c).
3. **Design numbers vs engine numbers.** Should the seed be calibrated so Day 1 roughly matches the mock-ups (76.4M revenue, 41.2M cash, 15 orders / 168M, 81% satisfaction, 92% capacity), or are they only illustrative? The Decision Room option deltas in the mock-up were invented and will change.
4. **Day 1 storyline.** The design shows three telegrams already on Day 1 (steel +20%, 8% discount demand, press line down). The spec slice runs report → big order arrives → supplier problem → decide → +30 days. Should Day 1 open calm, with events arriving as you advance, or start with all three telegrams on the desk?
5. **Desk contents.** The spec's Desk adds *overall health* and *employee turnover*, which the design lacks. The design adds capacity and cash-runway gauges. Add health and turnover in Milestone 2?
6. **Decision Room impact dimensions.** The spec lists financial / operational / customer / **people** impact plus a **custom decision** action. The design shows cash, margin, lead time, customer, risk (no people row) and three actions. Add a people row (overtime / turnover) and a 4th "custom decision" action? Is +30 days the "long term" horizon here, with 90/180/365 kept for Scenario Study?
7. **Time controls.** The design has only the "Advance day" bell. The spec needs day / week / month / pause. Fine to add week and month as a small brass dial or lever next to the bell?
8. **Advisor identity.** Use fictional names for department heads (spec) on the advisor notes, or role titles only (design)?
9. **Default locale.** The design started in English, while the spec defaults to TRY and Turkish users. Should the app default to TR, EN or the browser language?
10. **Layout width.** The mock-ups are fixed 1440px desk scenes. Is the minimum supported width 1280px desktop (scale the scene), or does it need a stacked tablet/phone layout?
11. **Fonts.** Self-host Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond and Courier Prime (offline, no Google request; recommended) or load them from Google Fonts?
